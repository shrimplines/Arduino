the stage where you use a lot of cardboard... feel free to make subsitutions for materials, this is just what I used. 

## ✧ electronics

* **microcontroller**: arduino uno
* **mini breadboard**: connect things together
* **stepper motors (x2)**: 28byj-48 5v steppers.
* **motor drivers (x2)**: uln2003 driver boards. (has leds, and usually comes with the stepper motors).
* **jumper wire variety pack**: a mix of **m-to-m**, **m-to-f**, and **f-to-f**.
* **buttons (x2)**: basic tactile buttons.
* **power**: a usb cable to plug into your computer.

## ✧ mechanical (cardboard supremacy)

* **spinner**:
* a stack of cardboard circles glued together for thickness.
* another wrapping of cardboard for the rim.
* cut a hole so the motor axle can pass through
* **rubber bands**: wrapped around the edge to give it enough friction to grip the page.

* **flipper**:
* a long, rectangular strip of cardboard to act as the main lever arm (double up so it won't bend).
* cut a hole so the motor axle can pass through

* **rig**:
* cardboard pieces and tape to adjust positioning.



## ✧ tools

* **box cutter**: for precise cardboard surgery (better than scissors).
* **scissors**: for quick cuts and "stripping" the 22/3 wires.
* **tape variety**: painter's tape for testing and electrical tape for wire joins.
* **organization box**: a dedicated box to keep all the tiny bits from disappearing.
